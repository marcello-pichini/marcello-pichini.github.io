Optional Google Analytics

This website uses Google Analytics 4 only after you choose “Allow analytics”. It measures page visits, referral sources, approximate location, device/browser information, engagement time, scroll depth, downloads and outbound link clicks. Google uses analytics cookies and identifiers to distinguish browsers and sessions. The reports help Marcello Pichini understand which pages and articles are useful.

Advertising storage, advertising personalisation and Google signals are disabled in the website tag. The website does not send names, email addresses or form contents as analytics events. Analytics cookies may last up to two years; this site remembers your consent choice in local storage for 180 days.

You can decline without losing access to any content. Use “Privacy settings” at the bottom of any page to change your choice. Declining after previously accepting stops Google tracking, removes this site’s Google Analytics cookies and reloads the page. Previously collected data is not automatically deleted by changing your preference.

Google processes analytics data using its infrastructure, which may involve processing outside your country. Read how Google uses data from sites that use its services and Google’s Privacy Policy.

Basic website analytics

Cloudflare Web Analytics also measures aggregate page views and site performance. It does not use analytics cookies or local storage to identify visitors. It remains active independently of the optional Google Analytics choice. Read about Cloudflare Web Analytics and Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy.

Hosting and external links

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub processes requests to deliver the site; see GitHub’s Privacy Statement. Links to LinkedIn, GitHub and Medium take you to services with their own privacy policies.
