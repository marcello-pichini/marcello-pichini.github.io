Digital assets.

I'm Marcello Pichini, a product leader bringing together digital assets, AI, and financial data.

At SIX Group, I work on crypto indices and financial data products, owning the roadmap for a broader portfolio of 300+ indices across crypto, global, Swiss, and ESG markets. I connect product strategy with quantitative research, engineering, and reliable market data.

More about my background +

My digital-assets work began at CheckSig, where I designed a crypto-asset benchmark and built the market-data infrastructure behind it. At IBM, I developed NLP and deep-learning systems for financial institutions. I hold master's degrees in Data Science and FinTech, with a data science thesis on Transformers for automated question answering.

I also co-founded ./cogita, a nonprofit where we make machine learning and generative AI accessible to broader audiences. Our courses have reached more than 12,000 learners.
